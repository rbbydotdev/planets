# Uranus

![Uranus](/assets/uranus.png)

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun. It is an ice giant, with an atmosphere rich in hydrogen, helium, and methane, which gives the planet its pale cyan color. It was the first planet to be discovered with a telescope, by William Herschel in 1781.

## A Tilted World

Uranus is unique in that it rotates on its side, with an axial tilt of about 98 degrees relative to its orbit. This causes extreme seasons, with each pole experiencing 42 years of continuous sunlight followed by 42 years of darkness.

### Quick Facts

- Diameter: 50,724 km
- Day length: 17.2 hours
- Year length: 84 Earth years
- Moons: 28 known (including Miranda and Titania)
