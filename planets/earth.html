Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life. About 71% of Earth's surface is covered with water, with the remaining 29% being continents and islands. It formed about 4.54 billion years ago.

A Living World

Earth's atmosphere is composed primarily of nitrogen and oxygen, and protects life from harmful solar radiation. Its single natural satellite, the Moon, stabilizes the planet's axial tilt and drives ocean tides.

Quick Facts

  • Diameter: 12,742 km
  • Day length: 24 hours
  • Year length: 365.25 days
  • Moons: 1 (the Moon)
