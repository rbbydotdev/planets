Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest in the Solar System after Jupiter. It is a gas giant, with an average radius about nine times that of Earth, yet only one-eighth the average density.

The Rings

Saturn is best known for its prominent ring system, which is composed mostly of ice particles with smaller amounts of rocky debris and dust. The rings extend up to 282,000 km from the planet, but are remarkably thin.

Quick Facts

  • Diameter: 116,460 km
  • Day length: 10.7 hours
  • Year length: 29.5 Earth years
  • Moons: 146 known (including Titan and Enceladus)
