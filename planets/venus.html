Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is often called Earth's "sister planet" due to their similar size and mass. It is the hottest planet in the Solar System, with surface temperatures around 465 °C, hot enough to melt lead.

A Hostile World

Venus's thick atmosphere is composed mostly of carbon dioxide, with clouds of sulfuric acid. This creates a runaway greenhouse effect, trapping heat and crushing any visitor under pressure 92 times that of Earth's atmosphere.
