# Mercury

![Mercury](/assets/mercury.jpg)

Mercury is the smallest planet in the Solar System and the closest to the Sun. Its orbit takes only about 88 Earth days, the shortest of all the Sun's planets. It is named after the Roman god Mercury, the messenger of the gods.

## Surface and Atmosphere

Mercury's surface is heavily cratered and resembles the Moon, indicating that it has been geologically inactive for billions of years. It has almost no atmosphere to retain heat, causing extreme temperature swings.

### Quick Facts

- Diameter: 4,879 km
- Day length: 59 Earth days
- Year length: 88 Earth days
- Moons: 0
