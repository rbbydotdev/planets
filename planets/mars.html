Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often called the "Red Planet" due to the iron oxide on its surface. It is a cold desert world with a thin atmosphere and is the most-studied planet beyond Earth.

Geography and Exploration

Mars is home to Olympus Mons, the largest volcano in the Solar System, and Valles Marineris, a canyon system that stretches over 4,000 km. Several rovers and orbiters are currently exploring its surface and skies.

Quick Facts

  • Diameter: 6,779 km
  • Day length: 24.6 hours
  • Year length: 687 Earth days
  • Moons: 2 (Phobos and Deimos)
