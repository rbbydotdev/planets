Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest in the Solar System. It is a gas giant with a mass more than two and a half times that of all the other planets combined. Jupiter has been known to ancient civilizations since before recorded history.

Bands and Storms

The planet's atmosphere is famous for its colorful bands of clouds and the Great Red Spot, a giant storm larger than Earth that has raged for at least 350 years. Jupiter also has a faint ring system and a strong magnetic field.

Quick Facts

  • Diameter: 139,820 km
  • Day length: 9.9 hours
  • Year length: 11.9 Earth years
  • Moons: 95 known (including Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto)
